Step 2: Prompt

You received an email from your friend, Jessie.

Hi Nicholas,

My cousins and I want to play an online game together this weekend. Should we choose a racing game, a football game or a building game? Why would you recommend that game? What can we do in the game? How long should we play?

Write back soon!

Jessie

Write an email to Jessie in about 80 words. Answer all four questions.

Sub-question with translations
1. Which game should we choose?Permainan manakah yang patut kami pilih?我们应该选择哪个游戏？
2. Why would you recommend that game?Mengapakah kamu mencadangkan permainan itu?你为什么推荐那个游戏？
3. What can we do in the game?Apakah yang boleh kami lakukan dalam permainan itu?我们可以在游戏中做什么？
4. How long should we play?Berapa lamakah kami patut bermain?我们应该玩多久？
